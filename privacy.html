Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short: the free version collects no data and sends nothing. SlidePace Pro still needs no account: about once a month, the extension checks only your license ID — never your name, your email or the content of your presentations. Payment is handled entirely by Paddle, our official reseller: we never see your bank details. This website sets no cookies itself; it measures its audience without cookies, and you can object to it.

The SlidePace extension

  • Free version: no data collected. The extension sends nothing to any server: no analytics, no telemetry, no account.
  • Pro trial: nothing sent. The 30-day Pro trial starts with one click and is recorded only in your browser; nothing is sent during the trial.
  • Local storage only. Your settings (duration, mode, warnings, sequences, size, position) and, for Pro customers, your license are saved in your own browser with its local storage.
  • Minimal permissions. The extension asks for the storage permission (to save your settings), the scripting permission and access to https://docs.google.com/presentation/* (to display the timer on Google Slides, including in a tab that is already open when the extension is installed or updated). For Pro customers only, an additional permission — requested when you activate Pro, and which you can decline — allows the license check, about once a month. The extension does not read the content of your presentations, your browsing history or your email address.
  • Extension stores. The Chrome Web Store (Google) processes data related to installing the extension as an independent data controller, under its own policies.

License check (Pro customers)

  • Data processed: your license ID (the order or subscription ID assigned by Paddle). It contains neither your name nor your email, but it is linked to your purchase at Paddle: it is pseudonymised data under the GDPR.
  • When: at activation, then about once a month; a check that gets no answer is tried again later, and you can start one yourself (“Verify now”). Free users are never concerned.
  • How: the ID is sent to our verification function, hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. It asks Paddle whether the license is valid (payment received, subscription active) and returns a signed answer. If the service does not respond, your license stays active for at least 30 days.
  • If you decline the permission: Pro keeps working for at least 30 days, then pauses with a message explaining why; the “Allow” button stays available at any time.
  • One-click activation: after purchase, the slidepace.com license page shows your license and passes it to the extension when you click “Activate in SlidePace”. The extension only accepts these messages from https://slidepace.com. On that page, the transaction number from your Paddle receipt is sent to our verification function to obtain your license, then removed from the page address. When you come back from the payment, this number is placed after the “#” sign of the address, a part that is never sent to the website host; if it is in an older link of the form “?txn=”, the requested address reaches the website host (GitHub Pages), which may record it in its security logs (see “This website”).
  • Purpose: confirming that your Pro license is valid, and nothing else (no advertising, no profiling).
  • Legal basis: performance of the license contract you entered into (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Retention: none. The ID is processed in memory for the duration of the request and then discarded: no database, logs disabled. Like any host, Cloudflare technically receives your IP address to route the request; we do not record it (for the sample of blocked requests, see “Abuse protection”).
  • Abuse protection: to prevent a bot from overloading our verification function, Cloudflare counts the requests received per IP address and blocks for a few seconds those that send too many (more than 10 in 10 seconds, or more than 60 per minute). A sample of the blocked requests (IP address, time, rule applied, and the country that Cloudflare associates with the address) is kept by Cloudflare for at most 31 days in our dashboard; we do not export it and only look at it in case of abuse. Our verification function never reads or derives your location. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in securing the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Processor and transfers: Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) acts as our processor (see “Transfers outside the European Union”). See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

This website

slidepace.com sets no cookies and shows no advertising; it measures its audience without cookies (see “Audience measurement” below). It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which, like any web host, may process technical data such as your IP address, your browser and the address of the pages requested to deliver the pages and ensure security (legitimate interest; retention set by GitHub). See the GitHub privacy statement.

The pricing page loads Paddle’s checkout script from cdn.paddle.com only when you choose a plan; payment then takes place in a Paddle window that uses its own cookies, strictly necessary for the payment and fraud prevention. The license page contacts only our verification function (see above).

Audience measurement

We measure the site’s audience with Cloudflare Web Analytics, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., which acts on our behalf as a processor under its data processing addendum (DPA). The script is loaded from static.cloudflareinsights.com.

  • Data processed: the page visited, the domain of the site you came from, your browser and operating system, the country derived from your IP address, and technical measurements of how fast the page displays. Your IP address is used only to derive the country; it does not appear in our statistics.
  • What is not used: no cookies, no identifiers, and address parameters (the part after “?”) are not recorded.
  • Purpose and legal basis: producing the site’s audience statistics, for our own use only, with no tracking from one site to another; legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing which pages are useful.
  • Duration: the statistics remain available for six months in Cloudflare’s tool.
  • Transfer outside the European Union: Cloudflare, Inc. is based in the United States; this transfer is governed by Cloudflare’s data processing addendum (DPA), which incorporates the European Commission standard contractual clauses.

This audience measurement is configured to be exempt from consent, based on our self-assessment against the criteria of the CNIL (the French data protection authority). You can nevertheless object to it at any time: your choice is saved as a simple flag in your browser’s local storage, which never leaves your device; as long as it is there, the measurement script is not loaded. It applies to this browser and disappears if you clear this site’s data. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, your visits are not measured.

Current status: JavaScript is disabled; your visits are not measured.

Email outreach

We sometimes write, one by one, to professionals (authors of articles, resource sites for teachers or trainers, education media) to introduce SlidePace.

  • Who processes the data: EURL PIERRE-PHILIPPE CONSEIL & COACHING (PPCC Studio), data controller (see below).
  • Data: your name or pseudonym, your professional email address, the page where we found it, the date of our messages and your replies; if you object, a fingerprint of your address (see “Retention”).
  • Source of the addresses: only addresses published by their owners for this kind of contact (a website’s contact page, the signature of an article). We never buy or rent mailing lists, and we do not write to private individuals without their consent.
  • Purpose: introducing SlidePace to you, in connection with your work. No mass mailing. Our outreach messages are sent from pierre.p@slidepace.com.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in making SlidePace known to the professionals concerned (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: at most 3 years from when we collected your address or, if you replied, from your last message to us. If you object, everything is deleted except two items, kept for the sole purpose of not writing to you again: a fingerprint of your email address (a code computed from your address, which lets us recognise it without keeping the address itself; it is still personal data, in pseudonymised form) and the date of your objection. They are kept for 3 years from that date, then deleted, including from earlier versions of the list, at our next quarterly review at the latest.

You also have the rights described in “Your rights (GDPR)”, including the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL (the French data protection authority) or with the data protection authority in your country.
