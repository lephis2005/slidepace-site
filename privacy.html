Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: the SlidePace extension collects no data at all, and this website has no cookies and no audience measurement.

The SlidePace extension

  • No data collected. The extension sends nothing to any server: no analytics, no telemetry, no account.
  • Local storage only. Your settings (duration, mode, warning time, size, position) are saved in your own browser with Chrome’s local storage and never leave your computer.
  • Minimal permissions. The extension only asks for the storage permission (to save your settings) and access to https://docs.google.com/presentation/* (to display the timer on Google Slides). It does not read the content of your presentations, your browsing history or your email address.

This website

slidepace.com uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and loads no resources from other websites. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which, like any web host, may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and ensure security. See the GitHub privacy statement.
