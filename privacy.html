Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short: the SlidePace extension collects no data at all, and this website sets no cookies; it measures its audience without cookies, and you can object to it.

The SlidePace extension

  • No data collected. The extension sends nothing to any server: no analytics, no telemetry, no account.
  • Local storage only. Your settings (duration, mode, warning time, size, position) are saved in your own browser with Chrome’s local storage and never leave your computer.
  • Minimal permissions. The extension only asks for the storage permission (to save your settings), the scripting permission and access to https://docs.google.com/presentation/* (to display the timer on Google Slides, including in a tab that is already open when the extension is installed or updated). It does not read the content of your presentations, your browsing history or your email address.

This website

slidepace.com uses no cookies and no advertising. The only resource it loads from another website is Cloudflare’s audience measurement script (see below). It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which, like any web host, may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and ensure security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Audience measurement

We measure the site’s audience with Cloudflare Web Analytics, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., which acts on our behalf as a processor under its data processing addendum (DPA). The script is loaded from static.cloudflareinsights.com.

  • Data processed: the page visited, the domain of the site you came from, your browser and operating system, the country derived from your IP address, and technical measurements of how fast the page displays. Your IP address is used only to derive the country; it does not appear in our statistics.
  • What is not used: no cookies, no identifiers, and address parameters (the part after “?”) are not recorded.
  • Purpose and legal basis: producing the site’s audience statistics, for our own use only, with no tracking from one site to another; legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing which pages are useful.
  • Duration: the statistics remain available for six months in Cloudflare’s tool.
  • Transfer outside the European Union: Cloudflare, Inc. is based in the United States; this transfer is governed by Cloudflare’s data processing addendum (DPA), which incorporates the European Commission standard contractual clauses.

This audience measurement is configured to be exempt from consent, based on our self-assessment against the criteria of the CNIL (the French data protection authority). You can nevertheless object to it at any time: your choice is saved as a simple flag in your browser’s local storage, which never leaves your device; as long as it is there, the measurement script is not loaded. It applies to this browser and disappears if you clear this site’s data. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, your visits are not measured.
