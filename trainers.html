Keep every part of your workshop on time

Presentations, exercises, debriefs: each segment has its own time. SlidePace shows the time left on top of your Google Slides, for you and for the participants.

For each segment

  • Set the duration of the current segment, then reset the timer for the next one.
  • The timer turns orange before the end and red at zero, then counts the overtime: you know exactly how late you are.
  • Add or remove a minute live when a discussion is worth it (+1 / −1 buttons or shortcuts).
  • Countdown or stopwatch mode.

Without breaking your flow

  • Keyboard shortcuts: Alt+Shift+S (start/pause), Alt+Shift+0 (reset), Alt+Shift+H (show/hide) — you never leave your slides.
  • Clicking the timer never changes the slide.
  • Visible in full screen; it keeps its time if you leave and restart the slideshow.

Trusted by default

  • No account, and the extension collects no data: nothing about you or your participants is sent anywhere.
  • Works with your clients’ decks as well as your own: nothing to add to the slides.

Get started

  1. Add SlidePace to Chrome.
  2. Set the first segment in the popup (for example 10 minutes, warning at 2 minutes).
  3. Start the slideshow, then reset and change the duration between segments.
