Frequently asked questions

Is SlidePace free?
Yes. The free version is complete: countdown or stopwatch, colour warning, keyboard shortcuts, size and position. A paid Pro version is in preparation; the free version stays free.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no account and no sign-up.
What data does SlidePace collect?
None. The free extension sends nothing to any server: no analytics, no tracking. Your settings stay in your browser. See our privacy policy.
Does the audience see the timer?
Yes: the timer is shown on the slideshow screen, the one the audience sees. You can hide it at any time with Alt+Shift+H.
Does the timer appear in presenter view?
No. SlidePace shows the timer on the screen your audience sees, during the slideshow. Google Slides’ “Presenter view” window does not show the timer. Nor does a presentation published to the web.
Which browsers does it work in?
Google Chrome. Microsoft Edge can also install extensions from the Chrome Web Store, and so can other Chromium-based browsers (Brave, Opera, Vivaldi): we have tested Edge; the others are not tested.
Does it work offline?
The extension itself needs no internet connection. Google Slides must be able to open your presentation.
Does it work with PowerPoint or Keynote?
No. SlidePace works with Google Slides presentations in the browser.
Can I change the keyboard shortcuts?
Yes. The defaults are Alt+Shift+S (start/pause), Alt+Shift+0 (reset) and Alt+Shift+H (show/hide). You can change them on the browser’s extension shortcuts page (link in the popup).
What happens when the time is up?
The timer turns red and keeps counting the overtime, so you know how far over you are.
Does clicking the timer change the slide?
No. Clicks on the timer never move your slides.
In which languages is it available?
English, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), German, Italian and Japanese.
