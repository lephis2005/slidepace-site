System requirements

  • Browser: Google Chrome, recent version, on a computer. Phones and tablets are not supported.
  • Google Slides: presentation opened in the browser and started in presentation mode (slideshow), full screen or not. PowerPoint and Keynote are not supported.
  • Where the timer appears: on the screen your audience sees. It does not appear in the “Presenter view” window (speaker notes), nor on a presentation published to the web (“Publish to the web” link or a presentation embedded in another page).
  • SlidePace Pro: an internet connection about once a month, to check the license (optional permission requested at activation).
  • Technical protection measure: the Pro license is a signed file checked by the extension.
  • Languages: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), German, Italian, Japanese.
