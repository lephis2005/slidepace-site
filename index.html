Stay on time when you present with Google Slides

SlidePace shows a clear countdown or stopwatch on top of your Google Slides presentation, in full-screen presentation mode. No account, nothing to install besides the Chrome extension, and no data collected.

Add to Chrome — it’s free See pricing
  • On top of your slidesThe timer stays visible in presentation mode, even in full screen.
  • Countdown or stopwatchSet a duration, start, pause and reset with the keyboard or on-screen buttons.
  • Colour warningThe timer changes colour a few minutes before the end, then again at zero.
  • Private by designNo account, no server, no analytics. Your settings stay in your browser.

How it works

  1. Add SlidePace to Chrome.
  2. Choose the duration, mode, warning time, size and corner in the extension popup.
  3. Start your Google Slides presentation: the timer appears on the screen.

Your data stays with you

SlidePace only asks for two permissions: storing your settings in your browser, and running on Google Slides pages (docs.google.com/presentation). It does not read your slides’ content, and sends nothing over the internet. Read our privacy policy.
