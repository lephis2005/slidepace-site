A classroom timer on top of your Google Slides lesson

Keep activities on schedule without leaving your slides. SlidePace shows a large countdown or stopwatch on top of the slideshow, so the whole class can see the time left.

For timed activities

  • Exercises, group work, reading time, warm-ups, quizzes.
  • A large size that can be read from the back of the room.
  • The timer turns orange before the end and red at zero: students know when to wrap up.
  • Add or remove a minute without stopping the lesson (+1 / −1 buttons).

Simple for teachers

  • Set it once in the popup: duration, warning time, size, corner.
  • Start, pause or reset with the on-screen buttons or the keyboard (Alt+Shift+S, Alt+Shift+0).
  • The timer stays on every slide and keeps its time if you leave and restart the slideshow.

Reassuring for your school

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • The free version of the extension collects no data about you or your students: nothing is sent to any server. With SlidePace Pro (optional, paid), only the license ID is checked, about once a month.
  • Minimal permissions: storing your settings in the browser, and access to Google Slides pages only, to display the timer there (including in tabs that are already open). For Pro customers only, optional access to api.slidepace.com, requested at activation, to check the license.

Get started in one minute

  1. Add SlidePace to Chrome.
  2. Click the SlidePace icon and set, for example, 10 minutes with a warning at 2 minutes, size “Large”.
  3. Start your slideshow: the timer appears in the corner of the screen.
Add to Chrome — it’s free

The free extension needs no account and collects no data.
