  1. Write to contact@slidepace.com with your order number (or the email address used for the purchase): we trigger the refund with Paddle, or

The same steps apply to exercise your statutory 14-day right of withdrawal (section 7 of our terms of sale).

The refund is issued by Paddle to the original payment method, within 14 days, for the full amount paid, including taxes. It usually appears within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. Pro features are deactivated after the refund, at the latest at the next license check.

On your bank statement, the purchase appears as PADDLE.NET* SLIDEPACE (PayPal shows PAYPAL *PADDLE.NET). Paddle is our official reseller: this is your SlidePace Pro purchase.

If you have a problem after 14 days, contact us anyway: we will look at each case fairly.

The legal guarantee of conformity includes the obligation to supply all updates necessary to keep the digital content or digital service in conformity.

The legal guarantee of conformity entitles the consumer to have the digital content or digital service brought into conformity without undue delay following the request, free of charge and without major inconvenience.

The consumer may obtain a price reduction while keeping the digital content or digital service, or may terminate the contract and be fully refunded in exchange for giving up the digital content or digital service, if:

2° Bringing the digital content or digital service into conformity is unjustifiably delayed;

Any period during which the digital content or digital service is unavailable while being brought back into conformity suspends the remaining guarantee until the digital content or digital service is supplied again in conformity.
