Refund policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

If you are a consumer, you have 14 days from the date of purchase to withdraw, without giving any reason, and obtain a full refund: one-time payment or first payment of an annual subscription.

Right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer, you have 14 days from the date of purchase to withdraw, without giving any reason, and obtain a full refund. This right applies to a one-time payment and to the first payment of a subscription; it does not apply to renewals (under the refund policy of our reseller, Paddle), unless that policy or the law of your country provides more favourable terms. Withdrawal ends your subscription and your Pro license. This right is described in section 7 of our terms of sale.

How to exercise it

  1. Use the withdrawal button in Paddle’s customer portal, if it is offered to you (link in your confirmation email, or paddle.net): Withdraw, or
  2. write to contact@slidepace.com from the email address used for the purchase (otherwise, we will ask you to confirm from that address; the date of your first message counts for the deadline): we arrange the refund with Paddle.

You may use the model withdrawal form in our terms of sale (section 7), but you do not have to.

The refund is issued by Paddle to the original payment method, within 14 days of your request, for the full amount paid, including taxes. It usually appears within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. Pro features are deactivated after the refund (for a subscription, once the subscription is cancelled), at the latest at the next license check.

On your bank statement, the purchase appears as PADDLE.NET* SLIDEPACE (PayPal shows PAYPAL *PADDLE.NET). Paddle is our official reseller: this is your SlidePace Pro purchase.

Annual subscription: cancellation

For an annual subscription, you can cancel at any time to stop the next renewal: Cancel your contract. You keep Pro until the end of the paid year. If you are a consumer, within 14 days of the first payment, withdraw instead: cancelling alone does not get you a refund. If you cancel after a renewal, you can ask for the cancellation to take effect immediately: we then refund the remaining period pro rata. If you have a problem, contact us: we will look at each case sympathetically.

Your legal guarantees

The legal guarantees of conformity and against hidden defects also apply: see the two “Legal guarantees” boxes (one-time payment and annual subscription) in our terms of sale, section 8.
