Two timers for Google Slides: what sets them apart

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Both are Chrome extensions that add timers to Google Slides, but they work differently. SlidePace is our extension; Slides Timer is published by ClayCodes. This page only lists public, dated facts, so you can choose the one that fits how you present.

Two different approaches

  • SlidePace shows one timer on top of the slideshow, without changing your slides. You set it in the extension popup.
  • Slides Timer turns text you type in your slides into timers, for example <<5:00->> for a five-minute countdown. Each timer belongs to the slide where you type it.

  • Choose Slides Timer if you want timers built into specific slides, clocks, scoreboards (PRO) or automatic slide changes.
  • Choose SlidePace if you want one timer above the whole presentation, without editing your slides, with a free extension that collects no data.
