Activate and manage your SlidePace Pro license

SlidePace Pro works without an account. Your purchase gives you a license key: a short signed text that the extension checks by itself. This page explains how to activate it, keep it active and find it again.

Activate Pro after buying

  1. After payment, the “Your SlidePace Pro license” page on slidepace.com opens and waits for confirmation from Paddle, our reseller (this can take up to a minute).
  2. The page shows your key. If the SlidePace extension is installed in this Chrome, click “Activate in SlidePace”: the license is activated in one click. Otherwise, click “Copy the key”.
  3. To activate by hand: click the SlidePace icon in the Chrome toolbar → “I have a license key” → paste the key → “Activate”, then accept the optional permission Chrome asks for right away (it lets the license be checked, about once a month). The window shows “Pro active”.
  4. After a one-click activation, or if you declined: click “Allow” in the SlidePace window and accept the permission Chrome asks for.

Keep your key (password manager or note): you need it on another computer or after reinstalling.

The license check

  • What is sent: only your license ID (the order or subscription ID assigned by Paddle), to our license check service. Not your name, your email or the content of your presentations. Nothing is kept. Details: privacy policy.
  • When: at activation, then about once a month. A check that gets no answer is tried again later, when you open the SlidePace window or restart Chrome.
  • What you see: “Pro active”, your plan and the date of the last check.
  • If you refuse the permission: Pro stays active for at least 30 days, then pauses with the message “Pro license to verify”. You can allow the check at any time (“Allow”, or “Verify now” when Pro is paused).
  • The free version and the Pro trial never send anything.

Use Pro on another computer

Your license covers all the computers you use yourself (Terms, section 6). On the new computer, or in another Chrome profile:

  1. install SlidePace from the Chrome Web Store;
  2. activate Pro with your key (“I have a license key”), or find it again on the license page with the transaction number from your Paddle receipt.

No account is needed and nothing has to be done on the old computer. SlidePace works in Google Chrome on a computer; phones and tablets are not supported.

You lost your key

  • Open the Paddle receipt email (check your spam folder too) and copy the transaction number, which starts with “txn_”.
  • Paste it on the license page: your key is shown; if the extension is installed, click “Activate in SlidePace”. In the SlidePace window, the “Find my license key” link opens that page.
  • No receipt any more? Write to contact@slidepace.com from the email address used for the purchase: we will send your transaction number back to that address.

End of the annual subscription

  • The subscription renews automatically every year; nothing to do in the extension: the next check extends your license.
  • To stop it: Cancel your contract (Paddle customer portal: enter the email address used for the purchase, then choose your subscription), or write to contact@slidepace.com.
  • You keep Pro until the end of the paid year. After that, the Pro features are turned off at a following license check (if this check runs when you open the SlidePace window or click “Verify now”, the window shows “This license is no longer active (subscription ended or purchase refunded).”).
  • If you cancel after a renewal, you can ask for the cancellation to take effect immediately: we then refund the remaining period pro rata.
  • Your Pro settings (alerts, sequences) stay saved in your browser and the free timer keeps working.

Refund

  • You get a full refund, no questions asked, if you ask within 14 days of each payment (first payment, annual renewal or one-time payment): write to contact@slidepace.com or use the link in your Paddle receipt. How to ask: refund policy.
  • After the refund, the Pro features are turned off at the next license check at the latest; the free timer stays available.

Offline, or if our server does not answer

  • The timer itself does not need the internet (Google Slides only needs to be able to open your presentation).
  • If a check fails in the background, your key is kept and Pro keeps working: the check is tried again later, by itself. If Pro is paused and “Verify now” shows “Can’t verify right now”, your key is still kept: try again later or from another connection.
  • Pro stays active for at least 30 days even if our server does not answer. With a one-time payment and the check allowed, Pro never pauses because our server does not answer.
  • If Pro is paused (“Pro license to verify”): connect to the internet, open the SlidePace window, click “Verify now” and accept the permission Chrome asks for. A company or school network may block our license check server (api.slidepace.com): try another connection.
  • Only an ended subscription, a refunded purchase or a purchase disputed with your bank turns a license off. A network or server outage, never.

Your computer’s date

The license carries the date of its last check. If your computer’s date or time is wrong (more than a day behind, or far ahead), the check cannot succeed and Pro may stay paused; SlidePace may then show “Your computer’s date seems to be behind” (recent versions) or “Can’t verify right now”.

  1. Set the date and time to automatic in your computer’s settings (“Date & time” on Windows and on Mac).
  2. Open the SlidePace window again; if Pro is paused, click “Verify now”.

Your key is kept: it is never removed for this reason.

Remove the license

“Remove license”, in the SlidePace window, erases the key from this browser only. Your purchase is not affected: you can activate it again with the same key.

Still stuck?

Write to contact@slidepace.com with your transaction number and a screenshot of the SlidePace window (without any payment information). We will never ask for a password or a card number.
